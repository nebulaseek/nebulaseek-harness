# Xingyunxunzhi Brand Asset Usage Guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

To maintain the long\-term healthy development of the Xingyunxunzhi ecosystem, avoid user confusion, and facilitate the retrieval and identification of related resources, we have established these specifications and hope that everyone will adhere to them:

- In your project's descriptive text, you may use "Xingyunxunzhi" to truthfully and accurately describe your project's relationship with Xingyunxunzhi, for example, "built on Xingyunxunzhi" or "compatible with Xingyunxunzhi\." Such descriptions comply with license requirements and help users understand your project's positioning\.
- If you wish your project name to reflect its association with the Xingyunxunzhi ecosystem, we recommend using the abbreviated "XYH" designation for naming, which is both clear and facilitates communication within the community\.
- When naming your project, please avoid using the full "Xingyunxunzhi" name or the 星云寻知 word and logo directly, so users are not misled about who publishes and maintains your project\.
- Additionally, please avoid using our brand materials in your promotions or presentations in a way that could cause misunderstanding, so as not to give users the false impression of official endorsement, cooperation, or authorization\.
